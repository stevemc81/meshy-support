Privacy Policy for Meshy

Effective date: 29th July 2026

Meshy (“the App”) is developed and operated by Steven McWee (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

We do not collect, store, or process personal data through Meshy.

  • We do not require user accounts
  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not use analytics tools
  • We do not track users
  • We do not collect device or usage data

Wallpaper generation, customisation, and image processing are performed entirely on your device.

2. How the App Works

Meshy generates wallpaper images directly on your device.

  • All wallpapers are created locally on-device
  • Your wallpaper designs and images are not uploaded to us or to any server
  • An internet connection is not required for wallpaper creation or customisation
  • An internet connection may be required to make or restore an in-app purchase through Apple

3. Photos Permission

Meshy may request permission to save images to your device’s photo library.

  • This access is used only to save wallpapers you choose to export
  • The App does not access, read, or upload your existing photos
  • Images are stored locally on your device only

You can manage or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer personal data to third parties. Meshy does not transmit your wallpaper designs or photo library contents to us.

5. Third-Party Services

Meshy does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.

Meshy uses Apple’s App Store and StoreKit to offer a one-time in-app purchase and to restore previous purchases. Apple processes purchase and payment information under its own privacy practices. We do not receive your payment card details, Apple Account credentials, or other personal payment information.

Apple may provide non-personal transaction and entitlement information needed to confirm a purchase or restore access. For more information, see App Store & Privacy.

6. Children’s Privacy

Meshy is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Security

We do not operate user accounts or maintain a database of Meshy users’ personal information. In-app purchase processing is secured and managed by Apple.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this document.
