Privacy Policy for Meshy

Effective date: 9th April 2026

Meshy (“the App”) is developed and operated by Steven McWee (“we”, “us”, or “our”). This Privacy Policy explains how the App handles information.

1. Information We Collect

Meshy does not collect, store, or process any personal data.

  • We do not require user accounts
  • We do not collect personal information
  • We do not use analytics tools
  • We do not track users
  • We do not collect device or usage data

All functionality of the App is performed entirely on your device.

2. How the App Works

Meshy generates wallpaper images directly on your device.

  • All wallpapers are created locally on-device
  • No data is sent to any servers
  • The App does not connect to the internet for its core functionality

3. Photos Permission

Meshy may request permission to save images to your device’s photo library.

  • This access is used only to save wallpapers you choose to export
  • The App does not access, read, or upload your existing photos
  • Images are stored locally on your device only

You can manage or revoke this permission at any time in your device settings.

4. Data Sharing

Because Meshy does not collect any data, we do not share, sell, or transfer user data to third parties.

5. Third-Party Services

Meshy does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services.

6. Children’s Privacy

Meshy is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

7. Security

Since Meshy does not collect or store personal data, there is no personal information transmitted or retained by the App.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected by updating the effective date at the top of this document.
